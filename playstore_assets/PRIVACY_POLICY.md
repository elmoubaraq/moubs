# Privacy Policy for Evergreen

**Last Updated: November 24, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **Evergreen** ("we", "our", or "the App"), developed by **Moubs Corps**, handles your information. Our primary principle is simple: **your documents belong to you, and your files stay on your device**.

---

### Summary
Evergreen does not collect, sell, or upload your scanned documents, PDF files, signatures, or photos to private backend servers. All PDF generation, scanning, OCR, signing, encryption, and conversions occur 100% locally on your device.

---

### 1. Document & File Privacy (100% Local Processing)
Evergreen is engineered as an on-device utility tool:
- **Documents, Scans, & Images:** When you scan documents, merge files, compress PDFs, extract text, sign contracts, add watermarks, or convert photos, all processing takes place locally on your device's processor and storage.
- **On-Device OCR (Optical Character Recognition):** Text extraction is performed using on-device Google ML Kit models. No images or extracted text are uploaded to remote servers for processing.
- **Signatures:** Any digital signatures you draw or stamp onto documents are saved strictly in your device's private app sandbox. We do not store or transmit your signatures.
- **Google Drive & Cloud Picker:** When you open a file from Google Drive or another storage provider via the system document picker, the file is read only to complete the requested action on your device. We do not obtain continuous background access to your cloud accounts.

---

### 2. Information We Collect Automatically
We do not collect personal identifiers such as your name, email address, or phone number within the core app. However, to maintain app stability and display ads, certain third-party libraries may process limited technical data:
- **Device & Diagnostics:** Anonymous diagnostic information such as device model, operating system version, screen resolution, app crashes, and performance metrics may be collected to help us resolve bugs.
- **Advertising Identifiers:** Non-personal identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / GAID) may be processed by Google Mobile Ads to serve relevant or non-personalized advertisements, prevent ad fraud, and generate ad analytics.

---

### 3. Third-Party Services & SDKs
The App uses trusted third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device in accordance with their respective privacy policies:
- **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob):** Used to display banner, interstitial, native, and rewarded ads. [Google Advertising Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- **Google Play Services:** Used to provide core Android APIs and service infrastructure. [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **Google ML Kit:** Used for on-device machine learning and text recognition. [ML Kit Terms & Privacy](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms)

---

### 4. Device Permissions We Request
To provide its core document scanner and PDF utilities, Evergreen requests the following permissions only when required:
- **Camera (`android.permission.CAMERA`):** Required only when you use the document scanner or capture ID photos. Camera images are strictly processed in-memory and saved only if you choose to export them.
- **Storage / Media Access:** Required to open documents you choose to import and to save exported PDFs to your device's storage.
- **Internet Access (`android.permission.INTERNET`):** Required strictly for ad loading (Google AdMob) and checking for network-dependent app updates.

---

### 5. Data Retention & Deletion
Because Evergreen does not store your files on external servers, you have total control over your data:
- You can delete any saved document, scan, or exported PDF directly within the **Files** tab inside the app.
- You can use the **Clear All Documents** option in Settings to wipe all locally cached documents at once.
- Uninstalling the application will remove all application sandbox data from your device.

---

### 6. Children's Privacy
Evergreen is a general-purpose productivity utility and does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.
