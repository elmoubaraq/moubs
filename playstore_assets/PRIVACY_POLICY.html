Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Evergreen ("the App") collects, uses, and shares your information when you use our mobile application and services.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in privacy first. Most documentation processing happens locally on your device.

  • Documents and Images: Your documents, scans, and images are processed locally on your device. We do not upload your personal documents to our servers unless you explicitly use a cloud-sharing feature.
  • Device Information: We may collect basic technical information such as device model, OS version, and unique device identifiers to ensure app stability and performance.
  • Usage Data: We may collect anonymized analytics data regarding how you use the app (e.g., feature usage frequency) to improve the user experience.

2. Data Handling and Storage

Your documents are stored in your device's local storage. We recommend using your own cloud storage providers (Google Drive, Dropbox, etc.) for backups, which are governed by their respective privacy policies.

3. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services that collect information used to identify you:

  • Google Play Services
  • AdMob (Google)
  • Firebase Analytics / Crashlytics

4. Permissions

The App requires the following permissions to function:

  • Camera: To scan documents.
  • Storage: To save and open PDF files.
  • Internet: To provide core app updates and optional features.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information. Since we store data locally, you can delete all app data by uninstalling the app or clearing the app cache in your device settings.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
